Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

How The Flygerians collects and uses information through this website.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

The Flygerians operates this website. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is used, email theflygerians@outlook.com.

Information we collect

When you use our contact or catering enquiry forms, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, event details, guest count and message.

We may also receive basic technical information from your browser and hosting provider, such as an IP address, browser type and the date and time of a request.

How we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to questions, prepare catering quotes, arrange services and communicate with you about your request. We use technical information to help keep the website secure and functioning properly.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Form provider

Our website forms are submitted through Web3Forms. Web3Forms processes the information submitted through the forms so that we can receive and respond to your enquiry. You can read more about their privacy practices on the Web3Forms website.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide our services, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email theflygerians@outlook.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are in the UK.

Cookies and external links

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies. It does use external services such as Google Fonts, and it contains links to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
