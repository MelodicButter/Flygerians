Our Story

Two sisters, one mission

The Flygerians was founded by two sisters, Jess and Jo, bringing the sweet taste of Nigeria to the streets of London. Since 2016, they've been on a mission to put Nigerian cuisine at the forefront of the food market.

Starting with street food and pop-ups at venues like Uber HQ and Dr Martens, the sisters spent years building a following one plate at a time, bold flavours, honest cooking, and a personality that stood out on London's street food circuit.

Today, the sisters have established a home base in Peckham at their bar, offering Londoners a proper sit-down taste of their vibrant and flavourful Nigerian dishes, alongside drinks and a lively atmosphere that channels the same energy as their early pop-ups.
